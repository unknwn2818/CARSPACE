The aim of DriveSpace is to make electric cars made easier to explore by presenting the main information in a simple and organised way. First-time EV buyers can check range, price, vehicle type and booking options without needing to visit several different pages. The website also supports users who are less confident with technology because the main tasks are shown clearly: browse vehicles, compare models, estimate finance and book a test drive. For wider background information, users can also read UK low-emission vehicle guidance.
